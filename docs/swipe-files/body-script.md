# Body Script Template

Use this to draft your ad body. Two columns: what you SAY (voiceover or on camera) and what we SHOW (b-roll, screen recording, text overlay, angle change). Every important line of SAY needs a SHOW cue.

This is the one part of the ad that does not change. You write it once, you film it once, and every hook you test swaps in over the top.

## Length target

- Body voiceover: about 3 minutes 30 seconds.
- Total with hook on the front: 5 minutes hard ceiling.
- Roughly 140 words per minute, so the body is around 480 to 525 words of VO total.

## Format

Fill in each section. Keep them as separate blocks so you can edit one without touching the rest. The hook lives in its own file ([hook bank](hook-bank.md)) so it can be swapped freely.

---

## Relatability

She has to feel "she gets me, she was me" inside the first 30 seconds. This is your story, not a generic story. Specific moment, specific feeling, specific shift.

| SAY (voiceover / on camera) | SHOW (b-roll / proof / text overlay) |
|---|---|
| *Your story beat. Who you were, what you were doing, the exact moment something shifted. First person.* | *Visual of old life. Scrubs, work parking lot, alarm clock, the desk you used to sit at. Pattern interrupt every 3 to 4 seconds.* |

## Content (the mechanism)

The teaching beat. The actual vehicle, how it works, why it's different. This is where you demonstrate. Show the dashboard, the funnel, the asset. Don't just claim "I have a business that runs without me," show it.

| SAY | SHOW |
|---|---|
| *Lead with the vehicle, not the income. Explain the mechanism in plain language: where the product comes from, how the system works, why it doesn't require trading more hours.* | *Screen recordings, the funnel itself, the dashboard, a clean visual of the loop. Hands doing the thing where possible.* |

## CTA 1 (primary)

First clear next step. Calm, inviting, logical. Say what the next thing is and demonstrate where the button is.

| SAY | SHOW |
|---|---|
| *"When you click below, you get the free training that walks through exactly how this works."* | *Cursor moving to the button. Screen recording of the landing page.* |

## Twist the knife

The cost of doing nothing. Honest, not manipulative. No fake urgency. Just the truthful picture of what the next 12 months look like if she keeps doing what she's doing.

| SAY | SHOW |
|---|---|
| *Paint the version of next year that's waiting if nothing changes. Specific images: the same shifts, the same Sunday dread, the same Monday math.* | *Sunday-night b-roll, alarm clock, the parking lot again. Close on her future self, not yours.* |

## CTA 2 (closer)

Same ask as CTA 1, stronger now because she's bought in. This is where the emotional weight pays off into the click.

| SAY | SHOW |
|---|---|
| *"Click below. Watch the training. I'll see you on the other side."* | *Final demonstration of the click. End frame holds on the landing page or a clean closing image.* |

---

## Checklist before you film

- [ ] Read the entire body out loud. Does it sound like you talking to one woman, or like a brand talking to a market.
- [ ] Time it. Body voiceover under 4 minutes.
- [ ] Every SAY has a SHOW.
- [ ] No income claims, no dollar figures, no "easy" or "passive."
- [ ] No em dashes. No banned words.
- [ ] Names one clear next step (the free training).
- [ ] Cuts cleanly without the Relatability beat (so the body can stand alone if needed).

When this passes, you're ready for [hooks](hook-bank.md).
