# Hook Bank Template

Use this to write your 15 to 20 hook variations. One body, many hooks. Each hook is a self-contained first 1 to 3 seconds of the ad. They all swap in over the same body.

## Before You Write a Hook

1. The body has to be drafted first ([body script template](body-script.md)). Hooks are last, not first.
2. Open your ICA notes. Every hook is written to one specific woman.
3. Read the rules below before drafting. They are non-negotiable.

## Hook Rules

- **Statement, not a question.** No "ever wonder why" or "what if I told you" openers. They sound like AI and they don't land.
- **Names her reality.** If the hook could be said to any audience, rewrite it.
- **No numbers.** No dollar figures, no income claims, no "30 days to" type framing. Meta scrutinizes this niche hard, and the rule keeps the writing sharp.
- **No second-person attribute call-outs.** "You're exhausted" and "if you're stuck in scrubs" both get the ad flagged. Use first person (your own story) or third person (a general situation) instead.
- **No banned words.** No queen, babe, sister, 6-figure, manifest, unlock, level up, game-changer, recession-proof, boss babe, escape the rat race, financial freedom, laptop lifestyle, or anything from the broader anti-AI-writing list.
- **No em dashes.** Anywhere. Commas, colons, periods, parentheses.
- **Length.** 1 to 3 seconds spoken. Usually one sentence, occasionally two short ones.

## The Angles to Pull From

You don't need 20 hooks on the same angle. You need 15 to 20 hooks across different angles so you can see which doors actually open. A few that tend to work well for this avatar:

- **Effort isn't the problem.** "Hard work was never the issue."
- **It's the vehicle.** "A salary is rented income."
- **More of the same won't fix it.** "A better-paying version of the same life is not the upgrade it sounds like."
- **Successful on paper, empty inside.** "I had the title and the respect. I was also sitting in my car after shifts, empty."
- **Identity and permission.** "Built for more than this. And done acting otherwise."
- **The life she wants.** "Imagine waking up with no alarm and a day that already belongs to you."
- **Not another side hustle.** "Most side hustles are just more hours in disguise."
- **The real cost is time.** "Money comes and goes. Time doesn't come back."

These are angles, not scripts. Your hook is your own line, in your own voice, on the angle.

## Narrow It Down

Once you have 15 to 20, read every one out loud. For each, ask:

- Does this sound like me, or like a generic ad.
- Would this stop her scroll specifically, or any scroll.
- Does it pass every rule above.

Cut anything that fails any of those. You want to end up filming 15 to 20 you actually believe in.

## Hand It Off

Before you film, get a second pair of eyes on the list. Voice check, compliance check, hook strength check. Better one tight list of 15 than a sloppy list of 20.
