# Hook test tracker

Before an ad earns a spot in your full [KPI tracker](kpi-trackers.md), you use this to pick your winning hooks. You run your batch of hooks against the same body and log the engagement numbers here, so the data tells you which first three seconds actually earned attention.

The link opens a "Make a copy" prompt. Click **Make a copy** and it lands in your own Drive as yours to fill in. The shared version is view-only, so everyone starts clean.

[Make a copy of the Hook test tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/11opibDetlxnYH1UqB28eldr0s96nPuPnDAJGCuPEjbA/copy)

## What it tracks

One row per hook, in three groups:

- **Engagement:** spend, impressions, CPM, 3-second plays, Hook Rate (target above 25%), thruplays, Hold Rate (target above 15%).
- **Click:** link clicks, CTR Link (target above 3%), CPC Link (target under $1.50).
- **Decision:** your verdict (keep or cut) and notes.

You type the inputs (spend, impressions, plays, clicks). The sheet calculates the rates and costs for you. The hooks that clear the targets are the ones you keep running. The rest you cut.

How hook testing works start to finish is in [Testing](../testing.md), and how to write the hooks is in [Hooks](../hooks.md).
