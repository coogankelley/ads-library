# Hooks

The hook is the first 1 to 3 seconds. It is the only part of the ad we swap and test. Get it right and a good body does the rest. Get it wrong and the best body in the world never gets watched.

This page is how to write hooks that work. Write yours in the [hook bank template](swipe-files/hook-bank.md).

## One Body, Many Hooks

We do not write a new ad every time. We write one body we trust and 15 to 20 different hooks that swap in over the top. We film all of them against the same body, then let the data tell us which hooks earned their spot. The hook is the only variable. (Why this matters is in [Testing](testing.md).)

So your job this stage is volume then quality. Write 15 to 20, then cut hard.

## What Makes a Hook Actually Work

A hook is not a slogan. It is a line that says "I see you" in the first 2 seconds. Three things keep showing up in the hooks that perform:

- **It names her reality.** Not "are you tired of the rat race." That is everyone. The hook that names the exact moment she felt it (the car after a shift, the 2am scroll, the Sunday-night dread) is the one that gets watched.
- **It is a statement, not a question.** "Ever wondered why..." is an AI tell and it does not land. Lead with something that actually says something.
- **It is specific.** Specific beats clever every time. The more precise the image, the more it feels like it is about her and not about a market.

## The Rules (Non-Negotiable)

These are baked in before you write, not checked after:

- **Statement, not a question opener.** No "ever wonder why," no "what if I told you."
- **Names her reality, in her language.** If the hook could be said to any audience, rewrite it.
- **No numbers.** No dollar figures, no income claims, no "30 days to" framing. This niche gets scrutinized hard, and the rule keeps the writing sharp.
- **No second-person attribute call-outs.** "You're exhausted" and "if you're stuck in scrubs" both get the ad flagged. Use first person (your own story) or third person (a general situation) instead. This is the big Meta rule. See [Compliance](compliance.md).
- **No banned words.** No queen, babe, sister, 6-figure, manifest, level up, game-changer, boss babe, escape the rat race, financial freedom, laptop lifestyle, or anything from the [compliance](compliance.md) list.
- **No em dashes.** Anywhere. Commas, colons, periods, parentheses.
- **Length.** 1 to 3 seconds spoken. Usually one sentence, sometimes two short ones.

## The Angles to Pull From

You do not need 20 hooks on one idea. You need 15 to 20 across different angles so you can see which doors actually open. The angles that tend to work for this avatar:

- **Effort is not the problem.** "Hard work was never the issue."
- **It is the vehicle.** "A salary is rented income."
- **More of the same will not fix it.** "A better-paying version of the same life is not the upgrade it sounds like."
- **Successful on paper, empty inside.** "I had the title and the respect. I was also sitting in my car after shifts, empty."
- **Identity and permission.** "Built for more than this. And done acting otherwise."
- **The life she wants (future pace).** "Imagine waking up with no alarm and a day that already belongs to you."
- **Not another side hustle.** "Most side hustles are just more hours in disguise."
- **The real cost is time.** "Money comes and goes. Time doesn't come back."

These are angles, not scripts. Your hook is your own line, in your own voice, on the angle.

## The Pattern Library

There is a deeper layer behind those angles: 24 hook patterns (Open Loop, Contrarian, Future Pace, Objection Flip, Confession, Sticky Analogy, and so on), each one a different psychological door into the same message. The way to use them: pick the **awareness level first** (for this avatar, Unaware and Problem-Aware only), then pick a **pattern**, then write the line in your own voice.

Not every pattern is safe for this niche. Keep to the ones that work without numbers, receipts, or fake urgency:

- **Use:** Open Loop, Contrarian, Future Pace, Objection Flip, Status Shift, Common Enemy, Confession, Failure Admission, Sticky Analogy, Curiosity Gap, How-To-Without, I Wish I Knew, Nobody Talks About. Before/After only with non-financial states. "If you're X" is great for the Andromeda call-out.
- **Avoid:** anything built on a hard metric, a proof screenshot, a dollar or percent figure, a countdown, an ultimatum, or borrowed authority. Those pull you toward income claims and manufactured scarcity, which break the rules and the [compliance](compliance.md) gate at the same time.

The single strongest angle for this avatar comes straight out of her [ICA](finding-your-ica.md): she has spent years fixing how she *feels* about her situation instead of changing the *vehicle* she earns through. True, not hype, and it works as a Contrarian or Common Enemy hook.

## Real Examples From The Next Wave

These are from our own locked hook set, so you can see the rules in action. Notice every one is a statement, names her reality, and is either first person or a general truth (never a second-person call-out of her job or feelings):

- *"Hard work was never your problem. You have more of it than almost anyone."*
- *"A salary is rented income. The day you stop showing up, it stops showing up."*
- *"I had the salary, the title, and the respect. I was also sitting in my car after shifts, completely empty."*
- *"Counting down to PTO is counting down your own life in small, approved doses."*
- *"Imagine booking a flight on a random Tuesday, just because you could."*
- *"Most side hustles are just more hours in disguise. This one's built to give hours back."*

What none of them do: promise a dollar amount, call out her job in the second person, use the word "easy," or try to sell in the first sentence. The hook earns 3 more seconds. That is its only job.

## Write, Then Narrow

1. Open the [hook bank template](swipe-files/hook-bank.md) and your audience notes.
2. Write 15 to 20. Keep the weak ones in the list while you draft.
3. Read every one out loud. For each: does it sound like me or like a generic ad. Would it stop her scroll specifically, or any scroll. Does it pass every rule above.
4. Cut anything that fails. End up filming 15 to 20 you actually believe in.
5. Get a second pair of eyes on the list before you film. Voice check, compliance check, strength check.

!!! tip "Let Claude do the first pass"
    Hand Claude your [ICA](finding-your-ica.md) and [conversion quadrant](conversion-quadrant.md) and ask it for 15 to 20 hooks aimed straight at her, using the angles and rules on this page. Then have it help you narrow: which ones name her reality hardest, which just repeat an angle, which trip a [compliance](compliance.md) rule. AI is fast at volume and at matching your avatar. You still own the voice check and the final cut, because a hook has to sound like you, not like a bot.
